Privacy Policy

How FreemiumPDF handles your data, your files, and your privacy.

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

FreemiumPDF ("we", "us", "the site") provides free online tools at freemiumpdf.netlify.app. This policy explains what information is collected when you use the site, how your uploaded files are handled, and the choices available to you.

Your files: client-side vs. server-side processing

FreemiumPDF is built so that the large majority of tools process your files entirely inside your own browser, using JavaScript and WebAssembly. In these cases your files are never uploaded anywhere — they are read, processed, and turned into a result on your own device, and nothing is transmitted to our servers.

Exception — PDF Password Protector: this specific tool encrypts your PDF using a server-side library, so the file you upload is temporarily sent to our server to add the password, then the original and the result are both deleted immediately after the response is sent back to you. We do not retain, back up, or inspect these files. If file privacy is your top priority, prefer tools marked as running in your browser.

Information we collect automatically

  • Usage data: standard web server/analytics logs such as pages visited, browser type, device type, approximate location (city/country level), and referring site.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: used for basic site functionality and, where enabled, by advertising and analytics partners (see below).

Advertising

FreemiumPDF is supported by advertising. We work with third-party advertising companies (such as Google AdSense) to serve ads when you visit the site. These companies may use cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies to collect information about your visits to this and other websites in order to provide advertisements about goods and services of interest to you.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendor cookies via the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out page.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use them to remember basic preferences and to support analytics and advertising as described above. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time; disabling cookies may affect how some parts of the site function.

Data we do not collect

We do not require account creation, and we do not ask for names, payment details, or other personal identifiers to use the tools on this site.

Children's privacy

FreemiumPDF is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Since FreemiumPDF collects minimal personal data by design, most requests can be resolved simply by contacting us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us via our Contact page or email hello@freemiumpdf.com.
